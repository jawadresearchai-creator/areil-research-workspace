# CITRUS-NET primary statistical results v1

Source: `CITRUS_NET_Exhaustive_Master_Data_Workbook_v3_FROZEN_POPULATED (1).xlsx`

Biological unit: Pair_ID / prespecified target Graft_ID. Repeated isotope samples and time points are repeated measures.

## G2 — functional transfer
Among 16 excavated candidate pairs, 12 were prospectively R4 and four were R2/R3 controls. All 12 R4 unions exceeded the frozen +3.0‰ transfer threshold; none of the four R2/R3 controls did.

Peak receiver Δδ18O:
- R4: mean 26.068‰, SD 3.434, 95% CI 23.886–28.250.
- R2/R3 controls: mean 0.938‰, SD 0.122, 95% CI 0.743–1.132.
- Difference: 25.131‰, 95% CI 22.947–27.315.
- Exact permutation P = 0.000549.
- Fisher exact for transfer-positive frequency (12/12 vs 0/4): P = 0.000549.

R4 time course mean receiver Δδ18O: 2 h = 8.539‰; 6 h = 26.068‰; 24 h = 10.813‰. Controls remained near background (0.615, 0.850 and 0.600‰, respectively).

Mean R4 receiver-soil Δδ18O at 6 h was only 0.367‰. Paired xylem-minus-soil difference averaged 25.701‰ (95% CI 23.395–28.006; exact Wilcoxon one-sided P = 0.000244). All isotope records and chamber leak tests used in the primary analysis were QC-pass.

## Hydraulic-asymmetry mechanism
Twelve R4 pairs were assigned to Wet-Dry, Wet-Wet or Dry-Wet states (n=4 each).

Peak receiver Δδ18O:
- Wet-Dry: 33.660 ± 3.686‰.
- Wet-Wet: 20.515 ± 4.197‰.
- Dry-Wet: 11.860 ± 1.416‰.

Exact permutation omnibus F = 43.538, P = 0.000173.

The pair-level donor-minus-receiver baseline stem-water-potential gradient was strongly related to peak receiver tracer enrichment:
- Pearson r = 0.913, P = 3.36e-05.
- Regression slope = 21.103‰ per MPa, 95% CI 14.464–27.742.
- Spearman rho = 0.837, P = 0.000682.

All three pairwise state differences had the minimum two-sided exact P = 0.02857 for n=4 vs n=4; after Holm adjustment across the three pairwise comparisons, adjusted P = 0.08571. Therefore the manuscript should emphasize the prespecified omnibus/continuous-gradient mechanism rather than claiming three separately significant pairwise state contrasts.

## G3 — severing versus sham causality
Twelve R4 pairs were randomized 6 sham and 6 severed.

Pre-manipulation G2 peak receiver enrichment was comparable:
- Sham mean 25.547‰.
- Severed mean 26.590‰.
- Difference 1.043‰; exact P = 0.623377.

After manipulation under the common Wet-Dry state:
- Sham post mean peak receiver Δδ18O = 28.120‰.
- Severed post mean = 0.473‰.
- Severed-minus-sham difference = -27.647‰, 95% CI -30.329 to -24.964.
- Exact permutation P = 0.002165.

Within-pair pre-to-post change:
- Sham: +2.573‰.
- Severed: -26.117‰.
- Difference-in-change = -28.690‰, 95% CI -32.754 to -24.626.
- Exact permutation P = 0.002165.

Post-manipulation transfer-positive frequency was 6/6 sham versus 0/6 severed (Fisher exact P = 0.002165).

Receiver-soil Δδ18O at 6 h remained near baseline and did not differ materially between sham and severed groups (mean -0.033 vs +0.137‰; exact P = 0.551948).

Receiver sap-flow responses were directionally smaller in severed pairs at 2 and 6 h, but the secondary time-point family did not remain below 0.05 after Holm adjustment (adjusted P = 0.07143 for 2 h and 6 h). These data should be presented as supportive rather than primary causal evidence.

Receiver stem-water-potential changes were less consistent than the isotope results and should not be described as hydraulic recovery.

## Manuscript inference
The primary causal claim should rest on four linked findings:
1. prospectively classified R4 unions show receiver tracer transfer whereas R2/R3 controls do not;
2. receiver soil remains near isotope background relative to the large xylem signal;
3. transfer magnitude tracks the donor-to-receiver hydraulic gradient;
4. severing the target union abolishes transfer relative to sham.

Anatomy supports the physical continuity of the R4 unions, while the public GSE263656/GSE255759/qPCR-supported molecular arm remains independent contextual evidence.
