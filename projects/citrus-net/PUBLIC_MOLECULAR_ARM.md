# CITRUS-NET independent molecular arm

Primary Sargodha Kinnow observations remain the causal evidence base and are not merged with public expression data.

Locked public resources:
- GSE263656 — citrus graft-union vascular RNA-seq.
- GSE255759 — citrus root water-deficit RNA-seq.
- Paudel et al. 2017, Scientific Reports 7:15429 — independent citrus root PIP qPCR/hydraulic-conductivity anchor.

Prespecified modules before numerical extraction:
1. vascular/lignification: LAC/laccase, class-III peroxidases, PAL, C4H, 4CL, CCR, CAD;
2. graft organization: AUX/IAA and SAUR;
3. hydraulic transport: PIP1/PIP2 aquaporins.

Claim boundary: these datasets provide independent molecular context only. They do not constitute qPCR, RNA-seq, or gene-expression measurements from the CITRUS-NET orchard trees.
