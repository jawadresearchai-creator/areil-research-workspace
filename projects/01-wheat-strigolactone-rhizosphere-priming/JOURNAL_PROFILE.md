# JOURNAL PROFILE: New Phytologist

## General Information
- **Journal:** *New Phytologist*
- **Publisher:** Wiley / New Phytologist Foundation
- **Impact Factor:** 8.3 (2024–2025)
- **Subject Category:** Plant Sciences (Q1, top tier)
- **Article Type:** Original Research Article

## Manuscript Structure & Limits
- **Title:** Concise and informative (<= 150 characters), describing main findings without vague buzzwords.
- **Summary:** Structured abstract <= 200 words divided into:
  1. Motivation / What is already known
  2. Methods
  3. Key Results
  4. Concluding synthesis / What this study adds
- **Key-words:** 5–8 terms in alphabetical order, avoiding words already present in the title.
- **Main Text Word Count:** <= 6,500 words (excluding Summary, Acknowledgements, and References).
- **Figures / Tables:** 6–8 display items total.
- **Reference Style:** Harvard / APA style; all citations must have active DOIs.

## Open Science & Data Policy
- Raw datasets, reproducible scripts, and statistical pipelines must be deposited in open repositories or provided in reproducible supplementary materials.
- All biological material sources and accession numbers must be explicitly stated.
