# RESEARCH QUESTION SPECIFICATION

## Decision Use
Provides empirical, mechanistic, and biometric evidence to establish whether phosphorus-starved wheat roots broadcast a biologically functional strigolactone cue capable of priming neighboring unstressed wheat plants for mycorrhizal colonization and phosphorus uptake, distinguishing between chemical solute diffusion and fungal hyphal network transport.

## Primary Research Question
Does phosphorus-starved donor wheat (*Triticum aestivum*) broadcast an apocarotenoid strigolactone signal across a 0.45 um microporous membrane that is perceived by the *TaD14* alpha/beta-hydrolase in neighboring Pi-replete recipient wheat to induce mycorrhiza-specific phosphate transporter *TaPT4*, accelerate *Rhizophagus irregularis* colonization, and enhance phosphorus acquisition in the absence of recipient tissue Pi exhaustion?

## PICO / PECO Specification

### Population
- **Host Plant:** Hexaploid bread wheat (*Triticum aestivum* L. cv. Bobwhite), wild-type (WT) vs. strigolactone perception mutant (*Tad14*).
- **Fungal Symbiont:** Arbuscular mycorrhizal fungus *Rhizophagus irregularis* (DAOM 197198 / 181602, 500 axenic spores per recipient rhizobox).
- **Substrate:** Washed quartz sand/vermiculite mix (1:1 v/v) with modified Long Ashton nutrient solution.

### Exposure / Intervention
- **Donor Phosphorus Regimes:**
  1. `P_Starved`: Low-Pi nutrient solution (2 uM KH2PO4, 200 uM KCl);
  2. `P_Replete`: High-Pi nutrient solution (200 uM KH2PO4);
  3. `P_Starved_Tis108`: Low-Pi nutrient solution (2 uM KH2PO4) supplemented with 1 uM Tis-108 (specific cytochrome P450 CYP711A/MAX1 strigolactone biosynthesis inhibitor).

### Comparator & Barrier Systems
- **M0_Solid Partition:** Solid polypropylene partition preventing solute diffusion, root contact, and hyphal penetration (negative baseline control).
- **M1_Membrane:** 0.45 um microporous hydrophilic polyethersulfone membrane permeable to dissolved root exudates and strigolactones, but excluding root hairs and AM fungal hyphae.
- **M2_Mesh:** 30 um woven nylon mesh permeable to solute diffusion and AM fungal hyphae (enabling Common Mycorrhizal Network formation), but excluding root penetration.

### Primary Outcomes
1. **AM Colonization (%):** Percentage of recipient root length colonized by *R. irregularis* at 28 days post-emergence (magnified gridline intersect method).
2. **Arbuscule Abundance (%):** Percentage of root length containing branched arbuscules.
3. **TaPT4 Relative Expression:** Relative transcript abundance of mycorrhiza-specific phosphate transporter 4 (*TaPT4*) measured by RT-qPCR (fold change relative to M0 baseline).
4. **Shoot Phosphorus Concentration:** Total shoot P content (mg P / g dry weight, spectrophotometric molybdenum blue method).
5. **Rhizosphere Strigolactone Concentration:** Exudate strigolactone concentration in recipient compartment pore water (pmol / g substrate, LC-MS/MS 5-deoxystrigol/orobanchol equivalents).
